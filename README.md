# Hole Anchor Gripper — Cam-Lever Expanding Lock

> **SolidWorks · Mechanism Design · CAD Animation · Multi-Use Application**

A cam-lever expanding anchor gripper designed to insert into holes and lock in place to lift loads. The rubber body expands outward when the lever is actuated, gripping the hole walls from the inside. Designed for industrial lifting applications and rock climbing protection.

## How It Works

1. **Insert** — the pointed rubber body is inserted tip-first into a hole
2. **Actuate** — the lever arm is pulled, driving the conical metal shaft downward through the rubber body
3. **Expand** — the conical shaft forces the rubber body to expand radially outward, pressing against the hole walls
4. **Lock** — friction and mechanical interference between the expanded rubber and the hole wall resist pull-out forces
5. **Release** — reversing the lever retracts the shaft, allowing the rubber to contract and the gripper to be removed

The rubber provides passive compliance — it conforms to irregular or slightly tapered hole geometries, maximising contact area and grip reliability.

## Applications

**Industrial lifting** — holes in concrete slabs, precast panels, stone blocks, and structural components can be lifted without external attachment points. A single gripper inserted into a cast or drilled hole provides a secure lifting point.

**Rock climbing** — the gripper functions similarly to passive climbing protection (nuts/hexes), but can be placed in drilled or naturally occurring cylindrical pockets in rock faces. The lever actuation allows positive engagement without hammering.

---

## Design Highlights

- Conical shaft geometry ensures progressive, controlled radial expansion
- Rubber compliance accommodates hole diameter variation (±tolerance)
- Lever geometry provides mechanical advantage — small actuation force produces large clamping force
- Pointed tip guides insertion into blind holes without visual access
- Self-locking under load — higher pull-out force increases radial grip pressure
